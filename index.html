Quick answers

Where is my data stored?

DepositLens stores property records and evidence locally on your iPhone. No DepositLens account is required.

How do I create a backup?

Open a sealed record and export its PDF or ZIP archive. Save the exported file to a destination you control.

What does storage cleanup remove?

Cleanup removes only regenerable thumbnails and temporary export files. Original evidence and sealed records are preserved.
